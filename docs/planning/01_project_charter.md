# Purpose

This document establishes the official charter for the Renite project.

The Project Charter defines the project's purpose, vision, objectives, governance, stakeholders, deliverables, and guiding principles.

This document serves as the highest-level planning document for Renite.

All contributors SHALL follow this document throughout the project's lifecycle.

---

# Project Overview

Renite is a secure, intelligent, and community-driven digital platform designed to improve the recovery of lost assets, support missing person cases, and strengthen emergency response through modern technologies and trusted collaboration.

The platform combines secure reporting, artificial intelligence, geospatial services, real-time communication, identity verification, and future-ready integrations into a unified ecosystem that serves individuals, organizations, and authorized public agencies.

Renite SHALL be developed as a scalable platform capable of supporting educational institutions, businesses, communities, and government partners.

---

# Vision

To become the most trusted digital recovery and public safety platform by enabling secure collaboration between communities, organizations, and authorities.

---

# Mission

To provide an intelligent platform that simplifies the reporting, discovery, verification, and recovery of lost assets while supporting missing person response through secure technology, responsible innovation, and community participation.

---

# Project Goals

Renite SHALL pursue the following primary goals.

## Goal 1

Provide a trusted platform for reporting lost and found property.

---

## Goal 2

Improve recovery rates through intelligent matching technologies.

---

## Goal 3

Reduce recovery time by enabling secure communication between owners and finders.

---

## Goal 4

Support missing person reporting and emergency response.

---

## Goal 5

Provide organizations with tools for managing registered assets.

---

## Goal 6

Maintain high standards of privacy, security, and data protection.

---

## Goal 7

Build a scalable architecture capable of future national deployment.
